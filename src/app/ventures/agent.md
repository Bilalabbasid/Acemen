## Key UI/UX Behaviors
- Hovering over a product card reveals the secondary angle/macro detail of the **exact same item**. If no secondary angle exists, it smoothly zooms the primary image without swapping to a different product.
- Footwear sizes (`EU 39-46`) and Outerwear sizes (`S-XXL`) are fully integrated into PDP, QuickView, Cart, and Wishlist.
